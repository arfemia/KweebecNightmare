A dependency-pin correction and a reward-payout fix on top of 1.1.0: the Ziggfreed's CommonLib
requirement now matches what this build actually needs, and a finished chase pays out its
authored reward for real.

- **Requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 1.4.0 or newer.** The install requirement had fallen behind
  what this build was actually compiled against; grab the current Ziggfreed's CommonLib alongside
  this release, or the mod will not load.
- **Chase rewards actually pay out now.** Finishing a round used to roll and preview a reward
  that never arrived; a completed chase now delivers its authored reward for real. Amateur,
  Hardcore, and Nightmare also now hand out a reward on a loss too, not only a win (a reward
  authored win-only still stays win-only).

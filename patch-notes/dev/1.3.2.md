A hotfix cut from 1.3.1 that builds and runs on Hytale Update 8 (0.8.0-pre.5 and later 0.8 builds): `ServerVersion` is `>=0.8.0-pre.0 <0.9.0`. The pins follow in lockstep, `ziggfreedCommonVersion` 2.5.3 and `perfectUtilsVersion` 1.3.2, and the manifest's `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon` floor moves to `>=2.5.3`, since the jar calls its `util/ZigScheduler`.

- **The round's timers** (the arena's objective re-pastes, the team-spawn respawn hop, the result hold, the revive settle and the overworld resync) run on CommonLib's `ZigScheduler`, since Update 8 removes `HytaleServer.SCHEDULED_EXECUTOR`.
- **The grove biomes on Update 8's world generator.** Every prop entry takes Update 8's `PropDistribution` (`Assigned` over `Positions`); the scatter grids become `BaseHeight`, `Scaler` and `Jitter2d` over `SquareGrid2d` at the same spacing; the ground finds become a `Locator` with a top-down `Linear` scanner over the same heights; random turns become `RandomRotator`; the column props become `Manual` props. The two debris clusters are written into the biomes as `Weighted`, `Union` and `Offset` props, since Update 8 cannot read the cluster prefabs' part names. Prefab paths, weights, seeds and terrain are unchanged; prop positions differ.
- **Knockback on Update 8.** The Gustbloom burst's knockback is Radial, Update 8's replacement for Point. The five creature templates' melee knockback says `Type: Angled`, the type Update 8's `NPC_Attack_Melee_Damage` gives it, with force 0 and its lift kept, the same feel as on Update 7.

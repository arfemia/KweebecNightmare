A fix release. Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.2.0 reads only ground the server already has loaded, and a
round reads ground in places nobody is standing yet, so on 2.2.0 a cave shrine could end up out of
reach and the exit platform could go missing. Each of those places loads its ground first now. Requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.1.0 or newer.

- **Fixed: a cave shrine could be out of reach on CommonLib 2.2.0.** The cave shafts are carved as
  the round starts, long before anyone walks out to them, and on 2.2.0 a shaft could carve at a
  fixed height whatever the ground above it was. A shaft whose entrance missed the surface left its
  shrine unreachable, and the gate opens only once every shrine is lit. Each shaft now loads its
  ground and reads the real surface. If that ground still has not loaded after a wait, the pass
  carves nothing and leaves the cave to the next one.
- **Fixed: the exit platform could land at the wrong height, or not appear, on CommonLib 2.2.0.**
  The mod loads the ground around the exit before it places the platform at gate-open, but it
  counted the game's chunks as 16 blocks wide (they are 32), so it loaded the wrong patch and the
  exit's own ground was never in it. It loads the right one now. Gathering at the exit always won
  the round, with or without the platform.
- **Fixed: hunters and the Warden placed on ground the server had not loaded.** The hunters' den and
  each hunter wave load their ground before a hunter goes down, and the Warden's rise at the gate
  does the same, so on CommonLib 2.2.0 they stand on the real surface. A wave whose ground needs a
  moment to load arrives a moment later, and so does its warning.
- **The corrupted Kweebec and the Warden wear the mod's own copy of their void glow.** Hytale's next
  update renames the game's own, so this keeps them looking exactly as they do now. The shrine's green
  fire lights through a call that update keeps too. This is still the build for the current Hytale
  release.
- On a server running CommonLib 2.2.0, the leaderboard's "your rank" footer shows your numbers, and
  a Warden that leaves its fight range counts as a lost fight with no kill credit. The gate still
  opens.
- For server owners: a failure while placing the hunters' den, or while raising the Warden or the
  grove's hunter encounter, now leaves a warning in the server log that names which one.
- **Requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.1.0 or newer**, beside Perfect Utils as before. If your server
  moves to CommonLib 2.2.0, move to this version with it: 1.2.0 on 2.2.0 has the cave and exit
  trouble above.

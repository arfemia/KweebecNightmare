Kweebec Nightmare for Hytale's Update 7. Install it when your server moves to Update 7, together with Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0; a server still on Update 6 stays on 1.2.1.

- **Added two achievements and their titles.** Win a Relight and Escape round on Hardcore for No Light to Follow and the title "the Unbroken". Help bring down the Blight Warden for The Warden Falls and the title "Wardenbane"; it counts for everyone who took a real part in the fight. Both wait in your achievement book under Kweebec Nightmare, and the titles are yours to show in the title picker as soon as you earn them.
- **Made for Update 7.** The server no longer flags Kweebec Nightmare at startup as made for an older version of the game. It needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, CommonLib's Update 7 release, and Perfect Utils.
- **The Lunger still lunges at full speed.** Update 7 changed how a charging creature gets up to speed, which would have made the Lunger ease into each lunge and slow down before the end of it. It still bursts at you at full speed and stops dead.
- **A quiet server log when a round starts.** The grove's title card is set up the way Update 7 expects, so starting a round no longer writes a warning to your server log. It looks the same as before.
- **Hunters always appear where the round puts them.** On Update 7 a hunter put down where no survivor was close by could be tucked away out of sight and turn up later, outside the round's control. The mod now makes sure that part of the grove is awake before a hunter goes down there.
- **The grove's throws deal real damage types.** The Emberbloom's burst is the game's own Fire, the Gustbloom's is its Wind, and a hunter's Void Spit is Void, from Ziggfreed's CommonLib, so a resistance to one of those counts against it, where all of them used to land as plain physical hits. The Moonbloom stays physical. An Emberbloom's burst still never hurts a fellow survivor, with or without MMO Skill Tree, and with it thrown mushrooms still train Artillery. If you wrote your own round preset, its `ThrowableDamage` now names the thrown bloom (`KweebecNightmare_Emberbloom`, `KweebecNightmare_Gustbloom`, `KweebecNightmare_Moonbloom`) where it named the old damage types, so move your values to those keys.
- **The leaderboards have a new look.** Survivors of the Grove, which `/kweebec leaderboard` opens, now starts with where you stand: your place, how many players are on the board and your own total, best score, best time and runs, or a line saying you have no ranked run there yet. The top three get gold, silver and bronze trophies, and your own row is lit up.
- **The queue and results screens show their numbers.** While you waited for a round, the queue screen showed "{0}" where the survivors gathered, the countdown and the wait should be, and the results screen at the end read "Total time: {0}". Both now show the numbers, such as "2/4 survivors" and "Total time: 4:32".

Win on Hardcore or help bring down the Blight Warden for two new titles, and the Emberbloom and Gustbloom now hit with the game's own Fire and Wind.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer and Perfect Utils. A server still on Update 6 stays on 1.2.1.

### New

- **Added two achievements, each with a title.** Win a Relight and Escape round on Hardcore for No Light to Follow and the title "the Unbroken". Help bring down the Blight Warden for The Warden Falls and the title "Wardenbane", which counts for everyone who took a real part in the fight.
- Both wait in your achievement book under Kweebec Nightmare. Show either title from the title picker as soon as you earn it.

### Changes

- **The blooms deal real damage types.** The Emberbloom's burst is now the game's own Fire and the Gustbloom's is Wind. A hunter's Void Spit is Void, from Ziggfreed's CommonLib. A resistance to any of them counts against it, where they all landed as plain physical hits before. The Moonbloom stays physical.
- An Emberbloom's burst still never hurts a fellow survivor, with or without MMO Skill Tree. With it, thrown mushrooms still train Artillery.
- Survivors of the Grove, the leaderboard `/kweebec leaderboard` opens, has a new look. It starts with where you stand: your place, how many players are on the board and your own total, best score, best time and runs, or a line saying you have no ranked run there yet. The top three get gold, silver and bronze trophies, and your own row is lit up.

### Fixes

- The queue screen shows its numbers, such as "2/4 survivors", where it showed "{0}" for the survivors gathered, the countdown and the wait.
- The results screen at the end of a round shows its time, such as "Total time: 4:32", where it read "Total time: {0}".

### For server owners

- If you wrote your own round preset, its `ThrowableDamage` now names the thrown bloom (`KweebecNightmare_Emberbloom`, `KweebecNightmare_Gustbloom`, `KweebecNightmare_Moonbloom`) where it named the old damage types, so move your values to those keys.

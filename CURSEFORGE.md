# Kweebec Nightmare

**A co-op horror minigame for Hytale. Relight the grove's shrines and escape before the Blighted Kweebec catches you.**

The Void has crept into the Emerald Grove, and the gentle Kweebec now hunt through a midnight that never ends. Play alone or with up to three others. Relight the corrupted shrines to open the Heartwood Gate, then hold the platform beyond it together until the grove lets you go. Every shrine you light makes the hunt faster and hungrier.

## How to play

1. Type `/kweebec` to join the gathering. Everyone who joins in the next 20 seconds goes in with you, and if nobody does, you go in alone.
2. Gather Moonbloom in the grove and offer it at a Blighted Shrine to relight it. A ritual holds you in place and makes noise the hunter can hear.
3. Get caught and you're cocooned in roots until a friend cuts you free.
4. Light every shrine and the Heartwood Gate opens. Gather on the platform beyond it and hold it together until everyone gets out. If the group breaks up, the hold starts over.

A round runs on Nightmare. For an easier night type `/kweebec --preset=amateur`, and for the hardest, `/kweebec --preset=hardcore`.

## Features

- A new grove every round, with shifting shrines, caves and corrupted ruins.
- Four blooms to throw. Moonbloom stuns the hunter, Gustbloom knocks it back, Mirebloom slows it and Emberbloom burns it with fire, never your fellow survivors.
- **The Blight Warden.** On Nightmare and Hardcore the corrupted Warden rises when the gate opens and fights in three phases on the game's boss bar, with Blighted Kweebec rising to its side at each change. It's tougher with a bigger party and shows on your map, and the gate opens the moment it dies. Emberblooms sprout around it so you can wear it down.
- Two achievements with titles. Win on Hardcore for No Light to Follow and "the Unbroken", or help bring down the Blight Warden for The Warden Falls and "Wardenbane". Both are in your achievement book under Kweebec Nightmare.
- Perpetual midnight, thickening fog, a heartbeat that quickens as the hunter closes in, and whispers in the trees.
- Collect your spoils when a round ends. Win well and you earn better loot.
- With [MMO Skill Tree](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mmo-skill-tree) installed, thrown blooms train Artillery.

## Install

Place the jar in your server's `Mods/` folder with [Ziggfreed's CommonLib](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/ziggfreeds-commonlib) (2.3.0 or newer) and [Perfect Utils](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/perfect-utils). It needs a Hytale Update 7 server. On Update 6, use 1.2.1.

Patch notes for every version are on [GitHub](https://github.com/arfemia/KweebecNightmare/tree/main/patch-notes).
